Temnorid: Master

Choose a trade. Do the work. Earn your name.

A warm medieval workshop where you learn seven trades with your own hands: raise and train a foal, verify and seal documents, forge, build mechanisms, cook, sew and prepare herbs. Short orders, direct mouse work, fair retries. The text of the game is in Ukrainian.

About

You are a young villager in a shared workshop. There is no walking around and no punishment: you pick a trade from the hub, take an order, do one to three short hands-on steps and are paid for the quality of your work. A wrong answer or a broken mechanism only means you are told why and can try again for free. One wallet, one level and one save cover every trade. A session takes a few minutes; all twelve orders and both trainings take about an hour.

Your workshop stays on your PC. An account is optional, and the game does not read or write anything in Lords or any other game. All names, seals and symbols are invented for the game, and the herb sets are decorative, not medicine.

Seven trades, twelve orders

Stable

Feed, groom, pet and rest a foal, then train it on an obstacle course and with command timing. It grows as it levels.

Scribe

Compare a document with its reference, trace a mark and press the right seal in the right place.

Forge

Heat the metal to the mark, strike three rings and choose the finish the order asks for.

Workshop

Place the parts in the right order and test the mechanism.

Kitchen

Choose the three right ingredients, heat the pot and stir in time.

Tailor

Pick the fabric, cut along the pattern and stitch in the green zones.

Herbalist

Choose two herbs, grind to the mark and label the bundle.

Shared progress

One wallet, a level, a rank in every trade, three upgrades, a journal and a safe local save.

How a session goes

  1. Open a trade from the hub (the Stable is a good first stop).
  2. Take an order and work through its numbered steps.
  3. Confirm to see the result: score, coins and experience, or the reason it was sent back.
  4. Spend coins on an upgrade, or pick the next trade.

Scope and limits

Version 0.2.0: seven trades, twelve orders, two horse trainings, three upgrades, a local save, up to four players in a shared workshop, Ukrainian text only, no gamepad.

Mouse, or touch on a phone (see Phones). Your progress is saved on your PC.

System requirements

Windows 10 or newer, 64-bit, a graphics card that runs Direct3D 11. The game is light; it has not been measured on a range of machines, so no frame-rate promises are made.

Playing with others

Choose Play with others in the main menu: one player hosts, up to three more join with the address it shows or an online code. The host keeps the order board and the shared wallet, level and horse, and pays every order after checking it; each player works on their own task, and two players never take the same order. Everyone sees who works on which trade. Trainings are for the host only. Online games are not saved on a guest's PC, so a guest's own game is never touched. If the host leaves, the game ends for everybody.

Phones

Android and iOS builds come from Unity Build Profiles. A finger works like the mouse (trace, seal, anvil), every key has an on-screen button, and the places (workshop and overview) are tabs on the bottom bar. They have not been tried on a device yet, and no phone build is published.

Updates

The Windows game updates itself from the newest GitHub release when it starts. You can turn this off in Profile.

Part of the world of Temnorid

The first time you press Play, a sign-in screen like the one in Lords offers Sign in with Google (on Windows, when it is set up on the device), Play as a guest or Play offline. You can sign in later from Profile, and a signed-in player starts with their Google name. The workshop plays the same with or without an account, and offline.

Master does not read or write anything in Lords: coins, level and ranks live only in Master's own save.
